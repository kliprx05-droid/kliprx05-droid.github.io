Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

kliprX is used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It has no other users.

What data kliprX uses

  • OAuth access to the owner's YouTube channels with two permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and read the channel's own video list (youtube.readonly).
  • The channel ID, channel title and titles of the channel's own uploads, used only to avoid uploading the same video twice and to check that an upload succeeded.

How it is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API. No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

kliprX uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. When access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
