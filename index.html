kliprX

kliprX is a private publishing tool used by one creator to upload and schedule short videos to their own YouTube channels: Behind The Tap, Behind The Price, Behind The Ordinary, Behind The Map, Behind The Vice, Behind The Throne, Behind The Grid, Behind The Field and Behind The Home.

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos the channel owner made, set their title and description, and schedule when they go public. It does not access anyone else's channel or data.
